Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short: Drumwell does not collect your content, and has no account and no tracking, and shows an ad only if you choose to watch one (see Ads). Everything you make stays on your iPhone, or in your own iCloud if you switch on the backup. Purchases and a few anonymous usage counters go through RevenueCat with an anonymous identifier (see Purchases).

The microphone

Drumwell asks for the microphone so it can hear each tap on your drumhead. It listens only while a drum's tuner is open on screen and stops when you leave it or the app goes to the background. The sound is analysed on your iPhone, as it arrives, to measure the pitch of each tap. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded.

Your drums and tunings

The drums you add, their readings, targets and saved tunings are stored on your iPhone, in Drumwell's own storage. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else (the optional backup below is yours to choose). Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. You can delete any drum or kit in the app, and everything is removed if you delete the app.

Backup and restore

Drumwell Pro can put all your kits, saved tunings, tuning history and any notes you wrote into one file. "Export Everything" lets you keep that file in Files or send it wherever you choose, and "Import a Backup" adds a file back to what is already on the iPhone. Where an exported file goes next is up to you.

You can also switch on "Keep a copy in iCloud". It is off until you turn it on. Drumwell then saves the same file, named "Drumwell Backup.json", in the iCloud Drive of your own Apple account each time you leave the app, and "Restore from iCloud" reads it back. Apple stores that copy in your account under Apple's terms and privacy policy. It is not sent to us, we cannot see or read it, and Drumwell has no server for it. Turn the switch off, or delete the file in the Files app, to stop or remove it.

Purchases

Drumwell Pro is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store, as a one-time purchase or a subscription. Drumwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it asks Apple whether you have Pro. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

To know, and to restore, whether this installation has Pro, Drumwell uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a processor. RevenueCat receives each in-app purchase you make, assigns the installation an anonymous app user ID, and stores that ID, the purchase history (product, date, price and store transaction IDs) and basic technical device data (OS version, app version, country or storefront). Apart from the anonymous counters described next, nothing else from Drumwell, such as your drums, tunings or any sound, is sent to RevenueCat or anywhere else. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Purchases and usage. Drumwell uses RevenueCat to keep track of purchases. With it, the app sends a few anonymous counters: how many times the Pro screen was shown and closed without buying, which screen it was opened from, how many times a soft Pro suggestion was shown, in which version of the app the rating prompt was shown, and how many times the optional ad was offered, started and completed. These numbers are used only to understand and improve how the app is offered. They carry no content from the app, no name, email or other identifier, and are not used to track you across other apps or websites. Nothing is sent in test builds.

To ask for deletion of the purchase record, write to baltajmn@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my Drumwell purchase data". Apple keeps its own record of the order under its own policies.

Ads (optional)

Drumwell shows an ad only when you choose to watch one: on the Pro screen there is a button to watch a short ad and get a second drum for 24 hours. Nobody sees an ad they did not ask for, and Pro owners are never offered it. The ad is served by Google AdMob. Google code is not started until you tap that button for the first time.

When you watch an ad, Google receives your IP address (and so your approximate location), device and app information, your interactions with the ad and diagnostics, to serve and measure the ad and to prevent fraud. No advertising identifier is used for tracking, and Drumwell never asks to track you. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland you are asked for your consent first, and you can change it later in Settings, under "Ad privacy choices". How Google uses this information is explained in How Google uses information from apps that use our services.

What Drumwell does not do

No account, no tracking, and no ads other than the one you choose to watch. The only third-party code in the app is the RevenueCat purchases library, which handles purchases and the few anonymous counters described under Purchases, and Google's ad library, used only for the optional ad. Drumwell works with the network turned off (the optional ad needs it); the purchase record and the anonymous counters are sent to RevenueCat when the device is online.

Children

Drumwell is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone, children included. The only thing that goes to RevenueCat is an anonymous installation ID, the purchase record and the anonymous counters (see Purchases). Ads are only shown on request and are limited to a general audience content rating.
