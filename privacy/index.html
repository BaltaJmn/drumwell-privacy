Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

In short: Drumwell does not collect, store or share any of your data. Everything happens on your iPhone.

The microphone

Drumwell asks for the microphone so it can hear each tap on your drumhead. It listens only while a drum's tuner is open on screen and stops when you leave it or the app goes to the background. The sound is analysed on your iPhone, as it arrives, to measure the pitch of each tap. It is never recorded, saved or uploaded.

Your drums and tunings

The drums you add, their readings, targets and saved tunings are stored on your iPhone, in Drumwell's own storage. Nothing is sent to us or to anyone else. Your iPhone's own iCloud Backup may include them, as it does for other apps. You can delete any drum or kit in the app, and everything is removed if you delete the app.

Purchases

Drumwell Pro is sold and managed by Apple through the App Store, as a one-time purchase or a subscription. Drumwell never sees your name, email or payment details; it only asks Apple whether you have Pro. Apple's handling of that data is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

What Drumwell does not do

No account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no third-party code that collects data. Drumwell works with the network turned off.

Children

Drumwell is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone, children included.
