Drumwell

Tune your drums lug by lug

Drumwell is a drum tuner for iPhone. Tap the head about an inch in from each tension rod: Drumwell shows the pitch at that lug, tells you which rods to tighten or loosen, and lets you know when the whole head is even. Then it reads the drum's own note from a tap in the center.

No account, no server. The sound is analysed on your iPhone as you tap and nothing is recorded or uploaded. Drumwell has no sign-up, no ads and no tracking, and it works with the network turned off.

How it works

Mute the other head (lay the drum on a towel, or rest a hand on it) and hold the iPhone a few inches above the head. Tap lightly by the lit lug and let it ring; the first time round Drumwell moves on to the next lug by itself. Every lug shows its pitch in Hz with an arrow up or down, and a rod turns green when its lug is within 5, 10 or 20 cents of the others. Each lug settles to one steady value: a reading that disagrees waits for a second tap before it replaces the last one.

For the drum's pitch, free both heads and tap the center. Pick a note as the target and see how many cents away the drum is, and where the resonant head sits against the batter.

Your kit

Snare, rack toms, floor toms and bass drum, batter and resonant heads. Save a drum's tuning and bring it back later as the target for every lug, or save the whole kit's tuning for a gig and recall it in one go.

Price

One drum is free for good, with everything it needs: the lug readings, the targets and the drum's pitch. Drumwell Pro adds more drums, more kits and saved tunings: $12.99 once, or a subscription of $7.99 a year or $1.99 a month in the US (prices vary by country). Subscriptions can be cancelled anytime in your Apple account settings.
